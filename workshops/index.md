# Upcoming Workshops

Educator Workshop
July 29-31, 2025
Applications Open March 10th

Do you teach in the geosciences and want to engage your students with real-world data and build genuine skills they can use in their careers? Authentic learning activities for undergraduate students can be difficult to find and time-consuming to generate. QGreenland invites educators to a dedicated workshop to adapt their resources into ones that use authentic, QGreenland geospatial data. The workshop will bring together instructors across disciplines to create learning resources which use Greenland-focused data that can then serve the larger community. This workshop is best suited for formal undergraduate or graduate educators. Learn more at [2025 Educator Workshop](https:)
